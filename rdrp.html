RNA-Dependent RNA Polymerase (RdRp)

Polymerase-centered interpretation of replication performance, mutation persistence, and antiviral relevance.

RdRp in the Viral Cycle

RdRp (nsp12) is the catalytic core of the replication-transcription complex and operates with cofactors such as nsp7 and nsp8. It drives genomic and subgenomic RNA synthesis and therefore directly shapes replication throughput.

Functional interpretation should include proofreading context (for example nsp14) and co-mutation patterns, since polymerase performance is system-level rather than single-site only.
