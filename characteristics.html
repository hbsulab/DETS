Structural and Mutational Analysis

Explore structural and mutational characteristics of SARS-CoV-2, including mutation-rate trends, codon usage bias, probability sequence density (PSD), and other sequence-level analyses. These detailed analyses reveal fundamental principles of viral evolution, adaptation strategies, and the mechanisms driving variant emergence. Key analyses include: Mutation Rate — temporal spike mutation-rate analysis across major variants, including quantitative interpretation and figure-backed comparisons. Probability sequence density (PSD) — PSD scaling analysis with pipeline overview, monthly distribution trends, and the time-gradient signal linked to variant emergence. Open-close dynamics — analysis of open-close conformational changes in SARS-CoV-2 proteins through molecular dynamics simulations. Landscape and network — integrated epistasis networks, punctuated dynamics, and adaptive coevolutionary landscapes across SARS-CoV-2 evolution. Codon Usage Bias — domain-resolved codon usage fingerprints with embedded figures and captions for full spike, NTD, RBD, CTD, and S2. RNA-Dependent RNA Polymerase (RdRp) — polymerase-focused context for replication fidelity, nucleotide selection, and mutation emergence.
