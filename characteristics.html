Structural and Mutational Analysis

This section explores the structural and mutational characteristics of SARS-CoV-2 through multiple complementary analytical lenses that collectively reveal fundamental principles of viral evolution, adaptation strategies, and the mechanisms driving variant emergence. Our Mutation Rate analysis presents temporal trends in spike protein mutation rates across major variants, combining quantitative interpretation with figure-backed comparative analysis. The Probability Sequence Density (PSD) analysis examines scaling relationships in sequence variation, including monthly distribution trends, pipeline methodology, and the temporal gradient signals linked to variant emergence.

The Open-Close Dynamics section uses molecular dynamics simulations to analyze conformational changes in SARS-CoV-2 proteins, revealing how structural flexibility influences function and mutation tolerance. Our Landscape and Network analysis integrates epistasis networks, punctuated dynamics, and adaptive coevolutionary landscapes to map the fitness landscape across SARS-CoV-2 evolution. The Codon Usage Bias analysis provides domain-resolved codon usage fingerprints with embedded comparative figures for the full spike protein as well as individual domains (NTD, RBD, CTD, and S2). Finally, the RNA-Dependent RNA Polymerase (RdRp) section provides polymerase-focused context linking replication fidelity, nucleotide selection preferences, and emerging mutation patterns to viral adaptability.
