Structural and Mutational Analysis

This section is organized into three subpages that group the structural and mutational analyses around the two spike-focused tracks and the polymerase track. Spike (S) consolidates mutation rate, PSD, open-close dynamics, and landscape/network analyses into one spike-centric entry point. Spike RNA collects the codon usage bias analysis for the spike genome context. RdRp remains the dedicated polymerase page for replication fidelity, nucleotide selection, and mutation emergence.

Use the subpages below to navigate into the grouped analyses. The existing detailed pages remain available from those subpages for direct comparison and figure-level inspection.
