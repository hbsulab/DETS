SARS-CoV-2 Evolutionary Dynamics

What is DETS?

The Deep Evolution Tracing System (DETS) is a comprehensive platform for tracking SARS-CoV-2 viral evolution, variant emergence, and global spread through high-resolution genomic epidemiology. Since SARS-CoV-2's emergence in late 2019, the virus has accumulated numerous mutations across its genome. Key variants such as Alpha, Delta, and Omicron have successively dominated global transmission, driven by changes in transmissibility, immune evasion, and virulence.

Our platform integrates time-calibrated phylogenetic trees, mutational signatures, epidemiological metadata, and structural biology insights to provide a detailed, interactive view of viral evolution. Researchers can explore clade dynamics, mutation frequencies, lineage replacement patterns, and spike protein structural changes across different geographical regions and time windows.

Highlighted Research Areas

Spike Protein Evolution

The spike glycoprotein is the primary driver of SARS-CoV-2 evolution, controlling receptor binding, immune recognition, and entry efficiency. Major variants carry coordinated spike mutations that balance transmissibility and immune evasion. We track spike-specific mutations, domain-level dynamics, conformational changes, and their phenotypic consequences across the pandemic timeline.

Variant Emergence & Fitness

Novel variants emerge through punctuated bursts of mutation followed by rapid lineage expansion when fitness advantages exist. We analyze mutation rate trajectories, epistatic interactions, and lineage replacement dynamics to predict variant behavior and pandemic trends.

Immune Landscape Dynamics

Omicron and its descendants demonstrate remarkable immune escape through spike RBD mutations that reduce antibody binding while maintaining ACE2 engagement. Our platform tracks immune-relevant mutations and their population prevalence across time and geography.
