Viral RNA

Genome-level view of RNA architecture, replication logic, and mutation flow across SARS-CoV-2 evolution.

Genome Organization

SARS-CoV-2 carries a positive-sense single-stranded RNA genome that includes ORF1ab, structural genes (S, E, M, N), accessory regions, and untranslated regions. This organization supports both immediate translation and downstream replication-transcription programs.

Tracking where substitutions accumulate across coding and non-coding regions helps separate conserved loci from rapidly adapting regions under host and transmission pressures.
