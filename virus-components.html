Viral Components

Explore core molecular components of SARS-CoV-2 and their roles in replication, structure, and viral fitness. RNA provides a genome-focused overview of sequence organization, mutation context, and coding regions. Spike (S) Glycoprotein presents a structural and functional perspective on receptor binding, fusion, and immune-facing sites. RNA-Dependent RNA Polymerase (RdRp) is a polymerase-centered page linking replication machinery to mutational dynamics. Nucleocapsid (N) Protein covers phase separation, RNA packaging, and condensate-driven regulation by the N protein.
