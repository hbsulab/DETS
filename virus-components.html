Viral Components

This section explores the core molecular components of SARS-CoV-2 and their essential roles in replication, structure, and overall viral fitness. The RNA page provides a genome-focused overview of sequence organization, mutation context, and the functional roles of different coding regions throughout the viral genome. The Spike Glycoprotein (S) page presents detailed structural and functional perspectives on receptor binding, membrane fusion mechanisms, and the immune-facing epitope sites that drive antibody interactions.

The RNA-Dependent RNA Polymerase (RdRp) page centers on the replication machinery itself, linking polymerase structure and catalytic mechanics to emerging mutational patterns and their evolutionary consequences. Finally, the Nucleocapsid (N) Protein page covers phase separation phenomena, RNA packaging mechanisms, and the condensate-driven regulation processes that organize viral genome assembly and packaging into virions.
