Evolution

Evolutionary Tracking and Analysis

This section integrates two complementary analytical tracks for comprehensive evolutionary understanding. The deLemus track provides a figure-backed overview of SARS-CoV-2 spike evolution by combining probability sequence density (PSD) analysis with local mutation context mapping (LMCM) methods, supplemented by monthly update panels and time-course mutation tracking. This approach captures both the statistical patterns of spike mutation accumulation and the temporal dynamics of variant emergence across the pandemic.

The Large Language Model (LLM) track adopts a model-oriented perspective, presenting analysis notes generated through LLM-Hyena workflows, enabling prompt-driven exploration of evolutionary questions, and supporting future method comparisons and validation studies. Together, these complementary systems provide multi-scale perspectives on viral evolution dynamics, from granular mutation-level analysis to broader patterns in lineage replacement and adaptive change.
