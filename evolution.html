Evolution

Evolutionary Tracking and Analysis

Evolution includes two focused tracks: deLemus for figure-backed mutation dynamics and monthly trend panels, and LLM Hyena for model-oriented analysis and future comparative workflows. deLemus & LMCM provides a figure-backed overview of SARS-CoV-2 spike evolution, combining PSD and LMCM analyses with monthly update panels and time-course mutation tracking. Large language model (LLM) - Hyena is a model-oriented page for LLM - Hyena analysis notes, prompt-driven exploration, and future method comparisons. Both systems provide complementary perspectives on viral evolution dynamics across different scales of analysis.

External resources

  • Microreact and UShER for alternative tree views.
